How it works

Retrieval-augmented generation

Instead of relying only on what a model learned in training, the chatbot looks up your content first, then writes the answer.

Ingest

Your knowledge sources are split into passages and converted into searchable embeddings.

Retrieve

When a user asks a question, Samway AI finds the passages most relevant to it.

Generate

A language model writes an answer using the retrieved passages as context.

FAQ

Common questions

What is Samway AI?

Samway AI is an organization building AI products for the AI era. Its flagship product is a RAG-based chatbot that answers questions from your own content.

What does RAG mean?

Retrieval-augmented generation. The chatbot retrieves relevant passages from your knowledge sources and uses them to generate an answer.

Why did the chatbot give a wrong or incomplete answer?

The information may be missing, outdated, or unclear in your knowledge sources. Add or update the relevant content and test again.

Do I need to retrain the chatbot when my content changes?

No model retraining is needed. Update your knowledge sources and the chatbot uses the new content once it is indexed.

Does Samway AI offer more than the chatbot?

Yes. The chatbot is the first of a growing set of AI tools. Contact the team to learn what is available.
