This guide walks you through creating a Samway AI chatbot, giving it your content, and putting it in front of your users. By the end you will have a working chatbot that answers questions from your own knowledge sources.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get access to Samway AI">
    Contact the Samway AI team to get an account. See [Support](/support) for how to reach us.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a chatbot">
    In your account, create a new chatbot and give it a name that reflects its purpose, such as "Customer Support" or "Product Help".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add knowledge sources">
    Upload documents or add content for the chatbot to learn from. Samway AI indexes this content so the chatbot can retrieve it when answering. See [Knowledge Sources](/chatbot/knowledge-sources).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test your chatbot">
    Ask the questions your users are likely to ask and check that answers are accurate and cite the right content. Add or refine sources if an answer misses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy">
    Add the chatbot to your website or app. See [Deploy Your Chatbot](/chatbot/deploy).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Start with a small, high-quality set of documents. Clean, up-to-date content produces better answers than a large, unorganized dump.
</Tip>
