Once your Samway AI chatbot is trained and customized, deploy it where your users already are. This page covers going live and improving the chatbot afterward.

## Before you deploy

- Test the chatbot with realistic questions.
- Confirm your instructions and welcome message are final.
- Make sure your knowledge sources are up to date.

## Go live

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your chatbot's embed details">
    Open your chatbot and find its deployment or embed settings. If you need help, contact [Support](/support).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to your site">
    Add the provided snippet or integration to your website or app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    Open your site and start a conversation to confirm the chatbot loads and answers correctly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Improve after launch

Review real conversations regularly. When the chatbot misses a question, add or improve the relevant knowledge source, then test again.
