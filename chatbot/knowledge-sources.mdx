Knowledge sources are the content your Samway AI chatbot searches before it answers. Adding the right sources is the single biggest factor in answer quality. This page shows how to add sources and keep them useful.

## Add a knowledge source

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your chatbot">
    Select the chatbot you want to train.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add content">
    Upload documents or add the content you want the chatbot to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for indexing">
    Samway AI processes and indexes the content. Once indexing finishes, the chatbot can retrieve from it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test">
    Ask a question the new content answers and confirm the response uses it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Supported file types and size limits depend on your plan. Contact [Support](/support) for details.
</Note>

## Best practices

- **Keep content current**: remove or update outdated documents so the chatbot does not give stale answers.
- **Write clearly**: short sections with descriptive headings are easier to retrieve accurately.
- **Avoid duplicates**: conflicting versions of the same information confuse retrieval.
- **Fill gaps**: when users ask questions the chatbot cannot answer, add content that covers them.
