The Samway AI chatbot is a conversational assistant that answers your users' questions using your own content. Use it for customer support, product help, internal knowledge lookup, or any place people need fast, accurate answers.

## Key concepts

- **Chatbot**: a configured assistant with its own name, behavior, and knowledge.
- **Knowledge source**: content the chatbot learns from, such as documents or web pages.
- **Conversation**: a session between a user and the chatbot.
- **Deployment**: where the chatbot is available to users, such as your website.
