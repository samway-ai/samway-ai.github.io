Customizing your Samway AI chatbot shapes how it speaks, what it focuses on, and how it looks to users. A well-configured chatbot feels like part of your product rather than a generic assistant.

## What you can customize

| Setting | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Name | How the chatbot introduces itself to users. |
| Instructions | Guidance on tone, scope, and how to respond, for example "Answer only questions about our products." |
| Welcome message | The first message users see when they open the chat. |
| Appearance | Colors and styling that match your brand. |

## Write effective instructions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Define the role">
    State who the chatbot is and who it helps, for example "You are the support assistant for our customers."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the scope">
    Tell it which topics to cover and what to do when a question is out of scope.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a tone">
    Describe the voice you want: friendly, concise, formal, or technical.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Ask the chatbot to say when it does not know an answer instead of guessing. This keeps responses trustworthy.
</Tip>
