These are answers to common questions about Samway AI and its RAG-based chatbot. If your question is not covered here, reach out through [Support](/support).

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Samway AI?">
    Samway AI is an organization building AI products for the AI era. Its flagship product is a RAG-based chatbot that answers questions from your own content.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does RAG mean?">
    Retrieval-augmented generation. The chatbot retrieves relevant passages from your knowledge sources and uses them to generate an answer. See [How RAG Works](/how-rag-works).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why did the chatbot give a wrong or incomplete answer?">
    The information may be missing, outdated, or unclear in your knowledge sources. Add or update the relevant content and test again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to retrain the chatbot when my content changes?">
    No model retraining is needed. Update your knowledge sources and the chatbot uses the new content once it is indexed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Samway AI offer more than the chatbot?">
    Yes. The chatbot is the first of a growing set of AI tools. Contact the team to learn what is available.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
